01 / The story

Some places are visited. Nepal is felt.

Nepal is where the world rises, slows down, and looks inward.

Walk through the living heritage of Kathmandu Valley, share tea with a mountain family, or watch first light touch the Annapurna range. Our local team creates considered journeys around the moments you will remember long after you return home.

02 / About us

Local people. Lasting journeys.

Born in Bhaktapur.
Guided by the mountains.

Explore Nepal began in 2012 with a simple idea: the best way to experience a country is through the people who call it home. Today, our team of guides, porters, drivers, and storytellers shares Nepal the way we know it: unhurried, honest, and full of warmth.

01

Rooted in Nepal

Every guide on our team was born and raised here, so you hear real stories rather than rehearsed scripts.

02

Small by design

We keep our groups small so every journey feels personal, flexible, and free of the crowds.

03

Travel that gives back

We hire locally, stay in family-run lodges, and support the communities that welcome us along the way.
